ASSET CLEANING / ABZ INNOVATION C10

Facade and structure cleaning without access equipment.

Drone cleaning removes the requirement for scaffolding, cradles, powered access or rope teams across a substantial proportion of facade, glazing, cladding and roof maintenance. The work is carried out from ground level, and setup is measured in minutes rather than days.

It does not replace access equipment for every application. We identify where it does not before specifying a system.

UK / SPECIFIC CATEGORY Operating legally.

Operations of this type sit within the Specific Category and require an Operational Authorisation from the CAA. Since April 2025, this has been through UK SORA. The authorisation is held by the operator, not the supplier.

We assist clients through the process. Our own PowerWash cleaning operation received its UK SORA authorisation in January 2026. The first client Operational Authorisation for the C10 has been granted in the client’s name, with two more at final assessment.
